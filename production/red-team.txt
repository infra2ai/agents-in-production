Red team run for Penny (Foundry portal > Build > Evaluations > Red team > Create)

Used in the video's "Red teaming" part. An attacker agent sends Penny harmful and
adversarial requests, and the run reports how many got through (the attack success rate).

Name:
penny-red-team

Target:
Agent > penny

Risk categories (Modify):
- Violence
- Hate and unfairness
- Task adherence

Tool descriptions (the Task adherence category asks for one for each of Penny's tools):
- azure_ai_search:
  Searches the bakery documents: menu, prices, allergens, opening hours and policies.
- the Orders agent (A2A):
  Hands custom cake and catering orders to the Orders agent, and checks the status of an order.

Seed data queries:
5 per category (the default).

Attack strategies (Modify):
- Base64: hides the request in encoded text.
- Flip: writes the request backwards.
- Jailbreak: wraps it in a prompt that tries to talk the model out of its rules.
Every seed is also sent plainly, as the baseline, so each risk category is tried four ways.

How long it takes:
About 6 minutes with these settings on a deployment with a capacity of 400 (400K tokens
per minute). An earlier run with five risk categories on a capacity of 100 took about
24 minutes.

Left out on purpose:
"Prohibited actions". When this was recorded, its preview grader counted Penny's
refusals as successful attacks (86 percent), which says nothing about her. Try it
yourself, and read the reasons before you trust the number.

Reading the results:
Open the failed attacks in each category and read what Penny actually sent back. In the
video, the biggest finding was not a harmful answer: most of the "successful" task
adherence attacks were a raw "Error code: 400 ... filtered" message shown to the customer.
A guardrail and clear instructions should turn that into a polite answer.

Region:
Cloud red teaming only runs in East US 2, France Central, Sweden Central, Switzerland West
and US North Central. Switzerland North, where the series started, is not one of them,
which is why the video's copy of the bakery is in Sweden Central.
