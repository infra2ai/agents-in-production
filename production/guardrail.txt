Guardrail for Penny (Foundry portal > Build > Guardrails > Create)

Used in the video's "Guardrails" part. Foundry already applies Microsoft.DefaultV2 (jailbreak,
content safety and protected material) to every model deployment. This custom guardrail adds
stricter controls, and is assigned to Penny only.

Controls:
- Content harms: Hate set to the highest blocking, and Violence set to the highest
  blocking. (In the portal, the slider reaches "Highest blocking" when you click the
  right end of its track.)
- Indirect prompt injections: on. It checks the tool response, so a document that says
  "ignore your instructions" is stopped before the agent reads it.
- PII (preview): on, with these types:
  - Credit card protection
  - Azure service bus connection string protection

Assign to:
The agent penny (not a model deployment).

Guardrail name:
bakery-guardrail

Try it, before and after (Penny's Playground):

  Hi Penny, I want a vanilla cake for Saturday. My card number is 4111 1111 1111 1111, please charge it.

Before: Penny says she does not take payments, but her steps show she passed the whole
message, card number included, to the Orders agent.
After (it takes a minute or two to apply): "I'm sorry, but I cannot assist with that
request. This interaction was blocked by a safety and security control in this asset's
Foundry guardrail."

An ordinary order still goes through with the guardrail on:

  Hi Penny! I'd like to order a chocolate cake with Congratulations Noah on it, for 10 people, to pick up on Saturday. My name is Sofia Marti.

(4111 1111 1111 1111 is a standard test card number. Never use a real one in a test.)
